GuardianAI Policy Engine
GuardianAI evaluates every AI action according to its potential risk and applies the appropriate security policy.
🟢

Low Risk

Score: 0–39

Policy: ALLOW

Low-risk actions can be performed automatically without human intervention.

AUTOMATICALLY ALLOWED
🟡

Medium Risk

Score: 40–79

Policy: HUMAN APPROVAL

Medium-risk actions must be reviewed by a human before they can proceed.

HUMAN REVIEW REQUIRED
🔴

High Risk

Score: 80–100

Policy: BLOCK

High-risk actions are automatically blocked to prevent unsafe operations.

AUTOMATICALLY BLOCKED

🔐 GuardianAI Security Principle

GuardianAI follows a risk-based decision model: safe actions are allowed automatically, medium-risk actions require human approval, and high-risk actions are blocked automatically.

LOW → ALLOW → MEDIUM → HUMAN APPROVAL → HIGH → BLOCK
